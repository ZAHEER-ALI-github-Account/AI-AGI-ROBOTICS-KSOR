**In short:** space is where robots go first. Rovers have driven across Mars
since 1997, a robotic helicopter has flown there, and a robotic arm works on the
International Space Station.

## Explanation

### Rovers on Mars

NASA's Mars Pathfinder mission demonstrated a new way to land safely on Mars
and delivered the first-ever robotic rover, Sojourner, to the Martian
surface.[^pathfinder] Perseverance, which NASA calls the most advanced robotic
explorer ever sent to Mars, is searching for signs of ancient microbial life
and collecting core samples of rock and regolith for potential pickup by a
future mission that would bring them to Earth.[^perseverance]

### Flight on another world

Ingenuity, carried to Mars beneath Perseverance, made the first powered flight
on another planet on 19 April 2021 and completed 72 flights in
total.[^ingenuity]

### Robotic arms in orbit

Canada's Canadarm2 is the robotic arm on the International Space
Station.[^canadarm2]

## Key points

- Sojourner was the first robotic rover on Mars.[^pathfinder]
- Perseverance searches for ancient life and collects samples.[^perseverance]
- Ingenuity made the first powered flight on another planet.[^ingenuity]
- Canadarm2 works on the International Space Station.[^canadarm2]
