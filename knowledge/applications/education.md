**In short:** generative AI can act as a tutor available at any time.
Controlled studies show that well-designed AI tutors can help students learn
more, but unguided chatbots can let students skip the thinking. UNESCO's
guidance asks for a human-centred, age-appropriate approach.

## Explanation

### Evidence from a randomised trial

A randomised controlled trial with college physics students compared an AI
tutor, built on research-based teaching practices, with an in-class active
learning lesson on the same content. Students using the AI tutor at home
learned significantly more — their median learning gains were more than
double — while spending less time on task.[^kestin]

The authors stress *design*: chatbots are generally built to be helpful, not to
promote learning, and their unguided use "lets students complete assignments
without engaging in critical thinking". They attribute the gains largely to
personalised feedback on demand and self-pacing, which are hard to sustain in
a typical classroom.[^kestin]

### Guidance for schools

UNESCO's first global guidance on generative AI in education proposes steps to
regulate GenAI tools, including protecting data privacy and setting an age
limit for independent conversations with GenAI platforms, within a
human-centred vision of the technology.[^unesco-genai-education]

### Learning at work

In a study of 5,179 customer-support agents, access to a generative AI
assistant raised productivity by 14% on average and by 34% for novice and
low-skilled workers, with suggestive evidence that it spread the best practices
of more able workers.[^brynjolfsson]

### Changing skills

The ILO estimates that one in four workers worldwide is in an occupation with
some exposure to generative AI, and concludes that transformation of jobs is
the most likely impact — which changes the skills people need.[^ilo-genai-2025]

## Key points

- A well-designed AI tutor more than doubled median learning gains in one
  RCT.[^kestin]
- Unguided chatbots can bypass critical thinking.[^kestin]
- UNESCO calls for privacy protection and age limits.[^unesco-genai-education]
- AI assistants help less-experienced workers most.[^brynjolfsson]
