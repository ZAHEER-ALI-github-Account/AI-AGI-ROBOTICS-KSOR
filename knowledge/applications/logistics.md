**In short:** logistics — moving and storing goods — is the largest market for
service robots. Warehouses run fleets of mobile robots coordinated by AI,
humanoids are being piloted for repetitive handling, and goods increasingly
move by drone and driverless vehicle.

## Explanation

### Warehouse fleets

Amazon announced in 2025 that it had deployed its one millionth robot,
describing itself as the world's largest manufacturer and operator of mobile
robots. Its robotics began in 2012 with one type of robot that moved inventory
shelves; today's fleet includes Hercules, which lifts up to 1,250 pounds,
Pegasus, which handles individual packages, and Proteus, its first fully
autonomous mobile robot.[^amazon-million]

### AI that coordinates robots

Amazon's *DeepFleet* is a generative AI foundation model that coordinates robot
movement across its fulfilment network, which the company says improves fleet
travel efficiency by 10%. Amazon likens it to an intelligent traffic system for
a congested city.[^amazon-million]

### The wider market

Transport and logistics was the largest class of professional service robots
in 2024, with 102,900 units sold (+14%), mostly mobile robots that move and
handle goods.[^ifr-service-2025]

### Humanoids and delivery

In June 2024 logistics provider GXO signed a multi-year Robots-as-a-Service
agreement for Agility's Digit humanoids, working alongside other automation at
a SPANX facility.[^agility-gxo] On the road, Waymo was providing over
150,000 autonomous rides a week in the US by the 2025 AI Index,[^ai-index-2025] and companies such as
Zipline deliver goods by drone.[^zipline]

### Jobs

Amazon says its robots handle heavy lifting and repetitive tasks alongside
employees and that it has upskilled more than 700,000 employees since
2019.[^amazon-million] The broader picture is in
[jobs and automation](../ethics-and-risks/jobs-and-automation.md).

## Key points

- Amazon deployed its millionth robot in 2025.[^amazon-million]
- AI models now coordinate whole robot fleets.[^amazon-million]
- Transport and logistics is the top professional service robot
  class.[^ifr-service-2025]
- Humanoids are in early commercial pilots.[^agility-gxo]
