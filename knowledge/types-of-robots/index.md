# Types of robots

* [Industrial robots](industrial-robots.md) - The programmable arms that build cars and electronics — how many there are, where they work, and the safety standards that govern them.
* [Humanoid robots](humanoid-robots.md) - Robots built in human form — why companies are building them, who the main players are, and how far they have really got.
* [Autonomous vehicles](autonomous-vehicles.md) - Self-driving cars and robotaxis — the levels of automation, how they perceive and decide, and what the safety data shows.
* [Drones and aerial robots](drones.md) - Uncrewed aircraft that fly with increasing autonomy — from delivery drones to the first helicopter on Mars — and the rules that govern them.
* [Medical and surgical robots](medical-and-surgical-robots.md) - Robot-assisted surgery, rehabilitation robots and the first experiments in autonomous surgery.
* [Service and home robots](service-and-home-robots.md) - Robots that work outside factories — moving goods, cleaning, guiding visitors and helping at home — and the safety rules for personal robots.
* [Swarm robots](swarm-robots.md) - Large groups of simple robots that cooperate through local rules, inspired by ants and bees — including the thousand-robot Kilobot swarm.
