**In short:** service robots do useful work outside manufacturing — in
warehouses, hospitals, hotels, shops and homes. Professional service robots
sold almost 200,000 units in 2024, and consumer robots — mostly floor and lawn
cleaners — close to 20 million.

## Explanation

### Professional service robots

The International Federation of Robotics reports for 2024:[^ifr-service-2025]

| Application | 2024 sales |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Transport and logistics | 102,900 (+14%) — the largest class |
| Hospitality (guidance, information, telepresence) | over 42,000 (−11%) |
| Professional cleaning | over 25,000 (+34%) |
| Agriculture | close to 19,500 (−6%) |
| Search & rescue and security | 3,100 (+19%) |

The IFR names staff shortages as a key driver for companies adopting these
robots.[^ifr-service-2025]

### Consumer robots

Consumer service robots grew 11% to close to 20 million units in 2024; robots
for domestic tasks such as floor cleaning and lawn mowing were by far the
largest group.[^ifr-service-2025]

### Toward general home robots

Cleaning one floor is a narrow job. A general home robot must handle many tasks
in cluttered spaces. Research systems show the direction: Mobile ALOHA learned
tasks such as cooking and serving shrimp, storing pots in a cabinet and calling
an elevator from about 50 human demonstrations per task.[^mobile-aloha] 1X
takes deposits for NEO, a humanoid marketed for household chores, which
includes a scheduled "Expert Mode".[^neo]

### Safety for robots near people

ISO 13482 sets safety requirements for personal care robots — robots that work
in close contact with people outside industry.[^iso-13482]

## Key points

- ~200,000 professional service robots sold in 2024, led by transport and
  logistics.[^ifr-service-2025]
- ~20 million consumer robots, mostly for cleaning.[^ifr-service-2025]
- General-purpose home robots are emerging from research.[^mobile-aloha][^neo]
- ISO 13482 covers safety for personal care robots.[^iso-13482]
