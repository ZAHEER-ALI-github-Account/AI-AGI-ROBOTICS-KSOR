## Timeline

| Year | Milestone |
| ---- | --------- |
| 1920 | Karel Čapek's play *R.U.R.* introduces the word "robot".[^rur] |
| 1950 | Alan Turing proposes the imitation game.[^turing] |
| 1955–56 | The Dartmouth proposal names "artificial intelligence"; the summer study runs in 1956.[^dartmouth] |
| 1958 | Rosenblatt publishes the perceptron, an early learning network.[^rosenblatt] |
| 1961 | Unimate, the first industrial robot arm, starts work at General Motors.[^unimate-ieee] |
| 1966–72 | Shakey, the first mobile robot to reason about its actions, is built at SRI.[^shakey] |
| 1986 | Back-propagation shown to learn internal representations in neural networks.[^backprop] |
| 1997 | Deep Blue beats world chess champion Garry Kasparov; Sojourner rover lands on Mars.[^deep-blue][^pathfinder] |
| 2005 | Stanley wins the DARPA Grand Challenge for driverless vehicles.[^stanley] |
| 2012 | Deep convolutional networks transform image recognition on ImageNet.[^alexnet] |
| 2016 | AlphaGo defeats a human professional at Go.[^alphago] |
| 2017 | The Transformer architecture is introduced.[^transformer] |
| 2021 | AlphaFold predicts protein structures with high accuracy; Ingenuity flies on Mars.[^alphafold][^ingenuity] |
| 2023 | RT-2 shows a vision-language-action model controlling a robot.[^rt2] |
| 2024 | The EU AI Act becomes law; Hopfield and Hinton win the Nobel Prize in Physics.[^eu-ai-act][^nobel-physics-2024] |
| 2025 | Amazon deploys its millionth robot; SRT-H performs a surgical step autonomously on ex vivo tissue.[^amazon-million][^srt-h] |
| 2026 | Boston Dynamics unveils a production Atlas humanoid; the second International AI Safety Report is published.[^atlas-2026][^iasr-2026] |

## Key points

- AI and robotics grew up separately — AI in software, robotics in factories
  and labs — and have been converging since the 2010s.
- The 2020s milestones are increasingly about *embodied* AI: models that
  control physical machines.[^rt2]
