**In short:** if AGI-level systems arrive, their effects could range from
accelerating science and medicine to disrupting work and concentrating power.
Every forecast here is a named source's view, not a certainty. Optimists and
cautious economists differ by orders of magnitude.

## Explanation

### Science and medicine — the optimistic case

Anthropic's CEO Dario Amodei imagines powerful AI as "a country of geniuses in a
datacenter", and guesses it could "at least 10x" the rate of biological
discovery, compressing "the next 50-100 years of biological progress" into
5–10 years.[^amodei-mlg] AlphaFold already shows AI changing a science: its
predictions cover virtually all 200 million known proteins.[^nobel-chemistry-2024]
The US government's Genesis Mission is an "SI-for-Science" initiative linking
federal agencies, national labs, industry and academia.[^si-gov]

### Work

Large language models could affect at least 10% of tasks for about 80% of US
workers, and half or more of tasks for about 19%.[^eloundou] Globally, one in
four workers is in an occupation exposed to generative AI, though the ILO
expects transformation of jobs rather than wholesale
replacement.[^ilo-genai-2025] Surveyed AI researchers put a 50% chance on all
occupations becoming fully automatable only by 2116.[^grace-survey]

### The economy — a cautious case

Economist Daron Acemoglu estimates that, on current evidence, AI will raise
total factor productivity by no more than about 0.66% over ten years, and warns
it could widen inequality.[^acemoglu-macro]

### Society and risks

Most surveyed researchers said "substantial" or "extreme" concern is warranted
about scenarios including misinformation, authoritarian control and
inequality.[^grace-survey] Safety researchers group catastrophic risks into
malicious use, an AI race, organisational accidents and rogue
AIs.[^hendrycks]

### An ageing society

Japan's Moonshot programme frames AI robots as a response to a declining birth
rate and ageing population, envisaging robots that support every aspect of
human society by 2050.[^moonshot-goal3]

## Key points

- Optimists foresee decades of scientific progress compressed into
  years.[^amodei-mlg]
- Exposure of jobs is wide; full automation is far off in expert
  surveys.[^eloundou][^ilo-genai-2025][^grace-survey]
- Some economists expect modest growth effects.[^acemoglu-macro]
- Misinformation, power concentration and inequality are leading
  concerns.[^grace-survey][^hendrycks]
