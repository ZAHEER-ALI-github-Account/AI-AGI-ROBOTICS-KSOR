robotics up to 2050, in date order. They are predictions and targets made by
the named sources — not facts, and not this record's own view. Forecasts in
this field have often been revised, sometimes sharply.

## How to read these

- **Surveys report beliefs.** The researcher survey's 50% date for
  machines beating humans at every task moved 13 years earlier between two
  surveys a year apart.[^grace-survey]
- **Trends are not laws.** METR presents its extrapolation with explicit
  caveats about whether it generalises to real-world tasks.[^metr]
- **Visions are not forecasts.** Dario Amodei's essay imagines AI compressing
  "the next 50-100 years of biological progress" into 5–10 years, explicitly as
  a guess about what could happen "if everything goes right".[^amodei-mlg]
- **Targets are plans.** Japan's 2050 goal is a research programme's ambition,
  not a prediction.[^moonshot-goal3]

## Key points

- Near-term robot forecasts (2025–2030) come from industry data and
  analysts.[^ifr-industrial-2025][^goldman-humanoid]
- AGI-level timelines vary from the late 2020s to beyond
  2100.[^grace-survey]
- Long-range "2050" statements are visions and targets.[^moonshot-goal3][^amodei-mlg]
